# Jury narrative v3 — SPIN compressed

This is the final narrative draft to approve before generating the owner's cloned
voice. It uses SPIN as an opening structure, then switches to visible proof.

## Situation

Phoenix has 4,288 active transit stops and a constrained field team cannot inspect
them all during an extreme-heat response.

## Problem

A heat map describes conditions but does not decide which stop should be inspected
first or explain why that choice is defensible.

## Implication

Without a repeatable workflow, scarce capacity can be allocated to priorities that
collapse under different assumptions. Field observations can remain disconnected
from the plan, and leadership receives a recommendation without a complete audit
trail.

## Need-payoff

Heat Priority Engine converts real FortyGuard evidence into robust priorities,
field missions, human-reviewed observations, a revised plan, and an auditable
decision package.

## Proof sequence

1. Real FortyGuard evidence and an explicit refusal to overclaim.
2. The verified default: three robust priorities across 324 scenarios at capacity ten.
3. One useful planner change: team capacity, with the updated queue shown transparently.
4. A field mission tied to the exact source run.
5. Human approval before plan version two.
6. Honest comparison with simple baselines.
7. Decision brief and machine-readable audit package.

## Closing promise

Answer three questions: where should we look, why this place, and can we prove how
the decision was made?

The value is not more heat data. It is a defensible way to act on it.
