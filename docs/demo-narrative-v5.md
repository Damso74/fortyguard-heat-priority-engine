# Jury demo v5 — 86-second decision story

This cut is deliberately shorter and outcome-first. SPIN structures the story,
but the narration never names the sales method.

## 0:00–0:10 — The decision gap

Phoenix has 4,288 transit stops. A heat map can describe heat; it cannot decide
where a constrained field team should act first or defend that choice.

## 0:10–0:28 — Real evidence, honest boundary

Show the full Heat Monitor surface and move once from 08:00 to 14:00. Establish
450 real FortyGuard cells, 27 Downtown Phoenix stops, preserved provenance, and
the explicit refusal to claim a persistent hotspot.

## 0:28–0:48 — Robust priority

Show the verified default plan. At capacity ten, three stops remain selected
across 324 scenarios; seven are assumption-dependent. Do not manipulate every
planner control.

## 0:48–1:10 — Human-controlled action

Open the first mission, record one field observation, submit it, and accept it
in Evidence Review. The plan advances only after human review.

## 1:10–1:26 — Auditable handoff

Show Reports & audit, point to package readiness and the decision brief, and end
on the promise: not another heat map, but a defensible way to decide where the
field team goes next.
